📋 How It Works

  1. Your request is submitted to the VRF smart contract on Stellar Mainnet
  2. The oracle fetches a drand quicknet beacon for the required round
  3. Oracle generates a BLS12-381 VRF proof bound to your context and the drand randomness
  4. The contract verifies the proof with two on-chain BLS12-381 pairing checks (~58M instructions)
  5. The verified random output (beta) is stored permanently on-chain
  6. Anyone can re-verify the result is unbiased and tamper-proof — no trust required
